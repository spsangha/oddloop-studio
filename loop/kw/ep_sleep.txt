Skip one night of sleep, and your face shows it.
Your eyelids hang, and your eyes turn red and swollen.
Dark circles form, your skin goes pale, and your mouth corners droop.
After 17 hours awake, your hand-eye skills match a blood alcohol level of 0.05%.
After 24 hours, they match 0.10%, over the US drunk-driving limit.
Scientists proved it by keeping 40 people awake for 28 hours.
And strangers rated those tired faces as sadder, too.
