Twenty adults ate meals like this while living in a research hospital for four weeks.
Each spent two weeks on ultra-processed meals and two weeks on minimally processed ones.
The menus offered about the same calories, and everyone could eat as much as they wanted.
On the ultra-processed meals, they ate faster and took in about five hundred more calories a day.
In two weeks on those meals, they gained about two pounds on average.
In two weeks on the other meals, they lost about two pounds on average.
