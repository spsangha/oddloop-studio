Take a cold shower every day for 30 days, and your body fights back.
On day one, you gasp, and your breathing speeds up.
Your heart races, and vessels in your skin squeeze tight, so your hands turn pale.
In a Dutch trial, 3,000 people ended their showers cold for 30 days.
After a few cold dips, the gasping and racing calm down.
They felt more energetic and took 29% fewer sick days off work.
But they didn't get sick any less.
