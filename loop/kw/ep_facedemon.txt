Every face this man saw turned into a demon.
Ears, noses and mouths stretched back, with deep grooves in foreheads, cheeks and chins.
Houses and cars looked normal, and he still knew who everyone was.
But on a screen, the same faces looked fine.
So scientists photographed a face and edited it until it matched what he saw.
An MRI found a small cyst beside his hippocampus.
It's called prosopometamorphopsia, and it turns every face he meets in person into a demon.
