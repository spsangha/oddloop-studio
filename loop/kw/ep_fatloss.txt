When he loses ten kilos of fat, he breathes out eight point four kilos of it.
That fat gets broken down into carbon dioxide and water.
To burn it, he breathes in twenty-nine kilos of oxygen.
Each resting breath out carries thirty-three milligrams of carbon dioxide.
The last one point six kilos become water, lost in sweat, urine and tears.
Most doctors, dietitians and trainers they asked thought fat turns into energy or heat.
So the fat he lost mostly left in his breath.
