He yawned so hard his jaw locked wide open.
His mouth wouldn't close, he was drooling, and the spots in front of his ears had sunk in.
X-rays showed both jaw joints had slid forward, out of their sockets.
In the ER, doctors sedated him and pushed twice, but it wouldn't budge.
The pain kept his chewing muscles clenched.
Next morning, under general anesthesia, his jaw slid back in easily.
Now doctors tell people like him to yawn with a fist under their chin.
