He breathed through his mouth for years, and it made his face longer.
It started with a blocked nose while his face was still growing.
With his mouth always open, his lower jaw rotated down and back.
His lower face grew taller, and the back of his face shorter.
His upper front teeth tipped forward and both dental arches narrowed.
In one study, nearly half of mouth breathers had a crossbite.
So his face finished growing longer, with his jaw angled down and back.
