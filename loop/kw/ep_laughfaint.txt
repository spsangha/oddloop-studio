He laughed so hard at a joke that he passed out.
He kept laughing, ha, ha, ha, until he ran out of breath.
Each laugh is a hard push of air that squeezes your chest.
That pressure slows the blood coming back to your heart.
Less blood reached his brain, his vision went dark, and he slumped onto the table.
Doctors call it laugh syncope, and he woke up within seconds.
It's very rare, but if laughing makes you dizzy, tell a doctor.
