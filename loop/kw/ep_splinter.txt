This is what your body does with a splinter you leave in.
First, blood rushes in, and the skin turns red and swollen.
White blood cells swarm the wood and try to eat it.
But it's far too big, so pus builds up around it.
The pressure can push the splinter back out of your skin.
If it can't, your body walls it off inside a firm little lump.
Doctors say: pull it out at the same angle it went in.
