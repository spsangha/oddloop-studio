He stretched his sore neck with his hand, and heard a pop.
Within moments, his left side went numb.
He could barely walk, tilting hard to the left.
At the hospital, doctors said he was having a stroke.
The pop had torn an artery in his neck, and a clot was blocking blood to his brain.
Doctors had twelve minutes to give a clot-busting drug.
Weeks later, he was walking again.
A stroke doctor says ninety-nine point nine percent of neck pops are fine.
